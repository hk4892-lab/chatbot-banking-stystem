🔒 SECURITY FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PII REDACTION (6 types)
  • Phone numbers      →  PII_PHONE_1
  • Email addresses    →  PII_EMAIL_1
  • PAN cards          →  PII_PAN_1
  • Aadhaar numbers    →  PII_AADHAAR_1
  • Card numbers       →  PII_CARD_1
  • Account numbers    →  PII_ACCOUNT_1

REFUSAL TRIGGERS
  • Low retrieval score (<0.2)
  • Sensitive keywords (balance, transaction, OTP, etc.)
  • Multilingual variants (बैलेंस, பணம், etc.)
